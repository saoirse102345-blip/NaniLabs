**What's actually working (2026):**

## ⚠️ Current Limitations (Still Unsolved)

From Sequoia research:
1. **Execution gaps**: GPT-4 can reason but struggles to execute its own plans
2. **Getting stuck**: Agents loop on same task or hallucinate steps
3. **No learning**: Agents don't remember past attempts
4. **Cost**: Recursive LLM calls add up
